MATHEMATICAL VERIFICATION:
===========================

Exponential Decay Formula:
  V(t) = V₀ × e^(-t/τ)

Where:
  V₀ = Initial voltage = 0.5V
  τ = Time constant = 10 seconds
  t = Time in seconds

Verification at key time points:

At t = 10 seconds (one tau):
  V(10) = 0.5 × e^(-10/10)
  V(10) = 0.5 × e^(-1)
  V(10) = 0.5 × 0.3679
  V(10) = 0.1839V (18.39%)
  Simulated: 0.1838V ✓

At t = 5 seconds (half tau):
  V(5) = 0.5 × e^(-5/10)
  V(5) = 0.5 × e^(-0.5)
  V(5) = 0.5 × 0.6065
  V(5) = 0.3032V (60.65%)
  Simulated: 0.3031V ✓
